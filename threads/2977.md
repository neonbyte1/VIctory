# Marvisak/VIctory#2977: Shaders: one back edge per block, for the dxil-spirv in Proton

## Description

A latch block that branches back to both its own loop's header and the outer loop's makes dxil-spirv recurse until its stack runs out in `LoopMergeTracer::trace_from_parent`, when the shader also reads other lanes. The pipeline creation never returns. I reported it as HansKristian-Work/dxil-spirv#380; the fix there (#381) isn't in any vkd3d-proton release yet, and the vkd3d-proton GE-Proton ships pins the affected dxil-spirv.

DXC never emits this shape, because its loops get a latch per back edge. So the translator now does the same:
- The outer back edge leaves through a latch block of its own. That block counts the outer loop's iterations like any other back edge, and aborts at the limit.
- The bitcode writer no longer folds an empty outer latch into a block that already branches back to another header.

**Translation** over the whole shader pack: the emitted counts per stage are unchanged, and 9 programs emit different DXIL. Those are the 9 that had such blocks:
- `InstanceCulling`: `CS_Prop_BatchNoCull`, `CS_Prop_BatchCullReflect`, `CS_Tree_BatchNoCull`, `CS_Tree_BatchCullReflect`
- `ShallowWaterSim`: `CS_PreSim`, `CS_PreSimWithDisturb`, `CS_SurfaceWaveSimUpdate`
- `WaterSimMgr:CS_UpdateDynamicFoam`
- `indirect_light_probes:CS_IrradianceCache_SpatialFilter_Separable`

Byte-identical everywhere else.

**Pipelines** (RTX 5090, driver 620.73.04, Wine 11.22, vkd3d-proton 3.1-83 from GE-Proton11-14, `victory translate --pipelines` on `CS_Prop_BatchNoCull`'s container, shader cache off):
- On `main` these programs are 64-lane waves, which a 32-lane host refuses before the converter runs. The hang appears once they're allowed in the console flow, as with #233/#439.
- On that base, without this change, there's no pipeline after 150 s (the native repro in dxil-spirv#380 overflows the stack in about 0.08 s). With it, the pipeline is created in 8 s.
- In game on our NVIDIA/Wine build (`main` with #233/#320/#439 and this change), every instance-culling pipeline the title asks for is made.

**Tests**: two new ones, `a_latch_branches_back_to_one_header` (lowering) and `an_inner_latch_keeps_the_outer_latch` (writer). `victory-shader` under Wine: 300 passed and 4 failed, against 298/4 on `main`; the same 4 Wine failures, fixed by #2968, #2973 and #1266. Clippy (with and without `diagnostics`) and `cargo xtask policy` are clean.

Not tested on Windows. There, DXC-shaped loops are simply what the driver usually sees, so I don't expect a change beyond the 9 programs.
