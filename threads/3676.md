## Description

**Stacked on #3197 (`pr/clamp-window`, `0c997d40`) — draft until it lands.** The diff against `0c997d40` is the one commit here and nothing else (`crates/victory-gpu/src/{indirect,draw,list,copystat}.rs`). #2472 is not needed; #3531 (our read-mark rule) is not needed but composes (see Overlap).

## What's wrong

#3197's windows refuse every indirect dispatch (`kind != DISPATCH` in `draw::execute_indirect`) and every execution whose shaders may store (`draw::may_store`). Each of those keeps a clamp of its own: a wait for all earlier work, a one-group compute pass, another wait, the execution, then the transition back that waits for its compute work. Two traced world frames: 1,618 of 1,822 indirect dispatches were recorded right after another indirect dispatch with nothing between it and them (no barrier, no barrier group, no pipeline sync, no other command); each had a clamp of its own and ran alone.

## The rule, and why it is safe

A window's pass clamps every record before the window's first execution runs. An execution that stores can write the records of a later one; on the console, a later execution reads its records when the command processor reaches it, which may be while an earlier one still runs — unless the title put a barrier or a pipeline sync between them. Records written by one execution and read by the next with neither between race on the console too, so the title does not do that. So, with a window open:

- after an execution that **may store**, the window takes the next execution only while **no barrier (`List::Barrier`), barrier group (`BarrierGroups`) or pipeline sync (`Sync`) came between** (`indirect::Run`, `indirect::stale_window`). A barrier after an execution that may store ends what the window takes: the next execution opens a window of its own, whose pass runs (and waits) after the earlier execution.
- barriers between executions that **do not** store leave the window as it is: nothing in it wrote.
- anything else ends the window exactly as before: any draw, dispatch or UAV clear that is not a joining indirect execution, a host library's work, the list's end (`end_window`).

Nothing about which executions run, in what order, or what they read changes; only where their records are clamped moves. The executions also run without our waits between them, as the title recorded them: in base each indirect dispatch was ordered against its neighbours only by our clamps' barriers, which the title never asked for (direct dispatches have none either).

## What changes

- `indirect.rs`: `windowed` takes `stores` and records it in a per-window `Run` (`stored`, `fresh`); `Run::takes` refuses the next execution after a barrier or pipeline sync following one that may store. `stale_window(list)` is the barrier/sync hook. The window's pass needed no change: it already clamps a dispatch record with the single clamp's bounds (x groups past 65,535, more than 2^20 groups in all, 64 threads a group, the list's budget in window order), and mesh/draw/indexed records as before.
- `list.rs`: `barrier`, `barrier_groups` and `sync` call `stale_window`. `sync` did nothing and records nothing; it is one cached-flag check now.
- `draw.rs`: `execute_indirect` asks only for the switch and a host pipeline; the kind and `may_store` are no longer part of the decision (a dispatch, and an execution that may store, join). `may_store` is asked only with the switch on, so an off build does the work #3197 promises (one flag check). An execution the window cannot take is clamped on its own as before, which ends the window.
- `copystat.rs` (diagnostics): `clamps per frame` now splits the windowed executions into indirect dispatches and draws that may store, and counts the executions that opened a window because a barrier or pipeline sync came after one that may store (`ClampWindowDispatches`, `ClampWindowStores`, `ClampStale`); `ClampStoring` is now only the draws that may store that a window refused (alignment, size, a full table or records, a fifth source buffer).

The switch is unchanged: `VICTORY_CLAMP_WINDOW=1`, off by default, and with it unset the added work is one cached-flag check per draw plus one per barrier, barrier group and sync.

## Overlap

- **#3197** (#3197's own head): this is its follow-up; it removes the two refusals #3197 lists under "Not addressed" — "the draws clamped alone because they may store" (narrowed, not removed: they join only while nothing but the window's executions comes between them) and "indirect dispatches keep the single clamp".
- **#2472** (hoist) composes without a change: a hoisted execution never reaches `windowed`, so the `Run` rule only applies to windows. On a tree with both, a storing execution that a prelude hoisted keeps its ordering as #2472 gives it; not measured.
- **#3531** (our read-mark rule) narrows `may_store` to "may write arguments". Composes: fewer executions count as storing, so fewer barriers end a window; this branch does not touch that predicate.
- **#2360-style driver watch, #2312** (spread dispatches): a record that needs a spread layout keeps the single clamp on a tree that has #2312; windowing those needs the window's pass to spread too (follow-up).
- **Machad3x's "storing draws can close a window"**: composes — his change lets a storing draw be a window's *last* member, this one lets it be an ordinary member while nothing but the window's executions comes between; both end the window before anything that follows is clamped.
