## Description

The console reads f32 denormals as zeros of their sign. DXIL `floor`, `ceil` and `frac` leave that to the host, and vkd3d-proton on NVIDIA does not advertise FP32 denorm flush, so `v_floor_f32`, `v_ceil_f32` and `v_fract_f32` of a denormal kept it. `v_fract_f32` of `0x80000001` gave `0x3f7fffff` (just under 1.0) instead of 0. The operand is now flushed on the bits first, as `max`/`min` and the float-to-int conversions already do.

**Tests** (Linux, Wine 11.22, vkd3d-proton 3.1-83 from GE-Proton11-14, RTX 5090, driver 620.73.04; run with `cargo xtask wine-test` from #1218):
- `emit::semantics_tests::rounding_matches_the_hardware` failed before on the 5090 and on the vkd3d-proton software path, 2 of 38 inputs each for floor, ceil and fract. It passes with this change.
- `victory-shader`: 299 passed, 3 failed, against 298/4 on `main` (63bcca4). The other 3 failures are Wine/vkd3d-proton gaps fixed elsewhere: #1266, and a PR of mine that follows.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` (with and without `diagnostics`) and `cargo xtask policy` are clean.

**Translation** over the whole shader pack: no program starts or stops translating. 11,157 programs emit different DXIL, all from the added flush. That's broad, so I ran it in game: two 7-minute runs on our NVIDIA/Wine test build, with no crash or new drops (556 against 558 dropped draws a frame, 86 against 90 indirect). Frame 1500 was still the loading screen in both, so this is not a visual comparison.

Not tested on Windows. On a host that flushes denormals already, the extra `and`/`select` is a no-op.
